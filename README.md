Ember & Tide is an original card duel that plays offline in a desktop browser. You face Vex, the Tidecaller, across a green felt table: both duelists hold 20 life, and each turn you draw 2 cards and gain 3 energy to play them. Cards belong to four suits told apart by colour and shape — Ember (red flame), Tide (blue droplet), Gale (green diamond), Stone (amber mountain) — and chaining two cards of one suit in a turn triggers a Surge (+2 power, plus a suit bonus). Vex announces her intent before she acts, so you can read her plan and counter it: Mirror Ward blocks her next attack, Quench shatters her shield, guards soak the damage. Reduce her to 0 life to win the crown.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
