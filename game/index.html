EMBER & TIDE

Outplay Vex, the Tidecaller — chain your suits, read her intent, take the crown.
🔥 Ember 💧 Tide ◆ Gale ▲ Stone
Both duelists hold 20 life. Each turn you draw 2 cards and gain 3 energy — play cards, then end your turn. Play two cards of one suit in a turn for a Surge (+2). Vex shows her intent before she acts — block it with Mirror Ward.
How to play. Click a card to select it, click again (or press Enter) to play it. Arrow keys pick a card, E ends your turn, M mutes, R restarts after the duel. Attacks deal damage (shields soak it first). Guards grant shield. Tricks: Siphon drains life, Quench shatters the rival shield, Kindle draws 2, Mirror Ward blocks the next attack. Surge: the 2nd card of a suit played in one turn gains +2 — Tide also heals 2, Gale draws 1, Stone adds 2 shield. Reduce Vex to 0 life to win; fall to 0 and the crown is hers.
